# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Visual Verifier adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Everyone participating in this project — through issues, pull requests,
discussions, or reviews — is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, accept technical criticism of
your work without treating it as criticism of you, and give the same
courtesy to others. Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning comments
are not acceptable in any project space.

## Project-specific expectations

This project verifies media that frequently contains faces, licence
plates, locations, and other personal data. Two rules follow from that:

- Never attach private or identifying media to a public issue or pull
  request. Synthesize a fixture, or describe how to generate one.
- Never present a `PASS` result as proof of anonymization, regulatory
  compliance, or legal safety. Overstated claims put real people at risk
  and are treated as a conduct issue, not only a documentation defect.

## Reporting

Conduct reports are handled privately. Use either channel:

- Contact the repository owner directly through their
  [GitHub profile](https://github.com/SAMtheROCKET). Profile contact
  details reach the maintainers without exposing your report publicly.
- Use GitHub's built-in
  [report abuse](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
  flow, which routes to GitHub Trust and Safety as well.

Do **not** open a public issue or discussion to report a conduct
violation. Public threads are for questions and proposals; they expose the
reporter and anyone else involved.

Two related processes are deliberately separate:

- Suspected security vulnerabilities follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
- Defect reports and feature proposals follow
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers
are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and will
explain their reasoning when doing so. Sustained or severe violations may
result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

The full escalation ladder and its definitions are described in the
Contributor Covenant text linked above.
